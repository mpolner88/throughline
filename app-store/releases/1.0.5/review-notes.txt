Throughline turns personal voice recordings into structured notes and a running list of tasks.

Use the review account entered separately in App Review Information. Choose Sign in, continue with email, and use those credentials. A free account is required to save and sync notes.

Review path:
1. On first recording, tap Agree and record, then allow microphone access if requested. This is the single explicit acceptance before sending a recording to the AI processor.
2. Record a short plan, such as calling the dentist tomorrow and picking up groceries today, then stop.
3. The capture tray shows saving and processing. When the note is ready, its tasks appear in Today, This week or Later.
4. Complete a task by its circle or a rightward swipe. Undo reopens it. The three-dot menu moves it; its source label opens the original note.
5. Notes contains saved note cards and full transcripts. Settings contains AI voice-note controls, optional agent access, feedback and account deletion. Existing notes remain readable when AI voice notes is off.

The guided first-use demo is also available before sign-in. This release does not enable Private Evaluation or automatic model training. No purchase is required.
