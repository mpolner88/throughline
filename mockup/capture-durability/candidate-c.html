The save receipt

When you stop talking, a quiet sheet rises from the recorder and draws the capture’s journey as a literal throughline — on your phone → saved to your account → in today’s plan → readable by your agent. The brand’s line becomes the product’s proof. Swipe it away and the journey continues on its own; failure is the one thing that waits for you.

The receipt is also where replay always lives: you can listen to what you said at any stage, not only after a failure.

The journey is drawn, not implied

  • Stage one is already true when the sheet appears: the audio is durably on the phone before anything else happens.
  • The vertical thread is the brand’s dot-line mark doing real work — each dot fills as its fact becomes true.
  • ▶ Play is always present: you can hear yourself back at any stage.

Sheet is a standard modal for VoiceOver; stages are a list, each “stage n of 4, label, state.” Close is a visible button — dismissal never requires the swipe.

What stays exactly as accepted

  • Home hierarchy, cards, and recorder states untouched. The receipt and status row exist only while a capture is in flight or needs attention.

Likely production mapping (for the eventual handoff, not implementation now)

  • New: durable capture outbox; `CaptureReceiptSheet` (extends the existing `ThroughlineMarker` dot-line language); status row above `bottomRecorder`; client capture ID for idempotent retry; local audio playback (AVAudioPlayer) for ▶ Play.
  • Changed: `stopAndUploadRecording` decomposes into keep → present receipt → background save; recorder unblocks after keep; `uploadError` banner and “saved” footer retire.
  • Open questions: server idempotency key; re-process endpoint; auto-settle timing after success (suggest ~1.2 s after “In today’s plan” fills, skipped under Reduce Motion in favor of immediate fade); receipt behavior when several captures are in flight (suggest the row aggregates and View shows the newest first).
