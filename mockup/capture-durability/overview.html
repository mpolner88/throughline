Never lose a spoken thought

The problem today: when you stop recording, the audio sits in temporary storage while it uploads. If that upload fails, the thought is gone — a small red error, no replay, no retry. And while it saves, the record button is locked for up to 40 seconds.

The fix all three candidates share: the audio is kept safe on your phone the instant you stop, the recorder frees up immediately, and the app always tells you the truth about where your thought is. The candidates differ in one thing only — where that truth lives on screen.

the truthful lifecycle — same in all three candidates
On your phone
Kept safe the instant you stop. Survives crashes and offline.
Saved to your account
Audio is server-durable — the phone can die now.
In today’s plan
Structured into the note and to-dos.
Readable by your agent
Only claimed once MCP is actually connected.
If anything breaks along the way: Save again (never creates a duplicate), ▶ Play your own voice back, or Discard with confirmation. Offline is a calm queue, not an error. The recorder is never blocked.
Candidate A
Born in place
The capture appears at the top of your notes — in the exact spot the finished note will occupy — and ages into it. Dashed border = not yet safe; solid = durable.

Candidate B · recommended
The recorder holds it
Nothing leaves the recorder until it’s safe. Captures dock in a one-line tray above the record button, and only enter Today’s plan as finished, durable notes.

Essence: a physical logic — the capture stays with the instrument that made it until it’s truly safe. The plan never contains anything fragile; a driver sees the state at arm’s length.
