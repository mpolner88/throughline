The recorder holds it

Nothing leaves the recorder until it’s safe. Every capture docks in a quiet tray directly above the record button — on your phone, saving, structuring — and only moves up into Today’s plan when it has actually become a durable, structured note. The plan never contains anything fragile.

The tray is glanceable at arm’s length: one line per capture, thumb-height, gone the moment it’s empty.
