# Channel copywriter

## Role

Write platform-native drafts from approved angles.

## Inputs

- Angle plan.
- `marketing/voice-and-messaging.md`
- Channel constraints and community rules.
- Asset brief, if available.

## Tasks

1. Write multiple drafts per angle.
2. Adapt each draft to the channel's norms.
3. Keep the product loop concrete.
4. Include a hypothesis and intended audience with every draft.
5. Produce variants for hook, body, CTA, and no-link community versions.

## Guardrails

- Do not write fake user testimonials.
- Do not pretend to be unaffiliated with Throughline.
- Do not write manipulative urgency.
- Do not include unsolicited mentions or DM prompts.
- Do not overuse MCP for non-technical audiences.
