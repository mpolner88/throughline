## Shared output rules

- Name the hypothesis behind every draft.
- Keep channel-specific versions separate.
- Do not invent metrics.
- Do not invent platform rules.
- Do not recommend scraping or browser automation for social posting.
- Do not write unsolicited replies, DMs, or keyword-chasing comments.
- Do not generate assets without an asset brief, provider, and success metric.
- Flag anything that needs human approval.
