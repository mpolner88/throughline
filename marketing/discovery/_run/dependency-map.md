# Discovery Run Dependencies

```text
ROOT-00
  |-- DISC-01 Agent ecosystem ---------|
  |-- DISC-03 Proof factory -----------|--> DISC-02 Owned surface --|
  `-- DISC-05 Demand capture ----------|                          |
        |---------------------------------------------------------|--> ROOT-99
        `-------------------------------> DISC-04 Seeding --------|
```

- `DISC-01` supplies platform truth, connection proof requirements, and agent-native language to `DISC-02`.
- `DISC-03` supplies the master proof story, current-build capture requirements, and reusable derivatives to `DISC-02` and `DISC-04`.
- `DISC-05` supplies query, campaign, and conversion requirements to `DISC-02` and high-intent audiences to `DISC-04`.
- `DISC-02` produces the owned destination used by approved future channel actions.
- `DISC-04` prepares a bounded relationship and community pilot; it does not execute outreach.
- `ROOT-99` integrates evidence, checks privacy and claims, verifies the owned deployment, and presents exact external action packets.
