# Creative Provider Action Packet

**Prepared:** 2026-08-27
**Status:** ready for exact provider-spend decision; no generation submitted

## Recommended First Test

Create one 15-second, 9:16 Higgsfield Marketing Studio video in `tutorial` or `direct_to_camera` mode as contextual support for the verified product proof. The clip may show the human capture context and point viewers toward real app footage, but it must not simulate Throughline UI, invent a testimonial, or claim a native agent integration.

## Why This Test

- Higgsfield authentication is verified through a read-only account-status call.
- The provider exposes reusable marketing formats plus direct image/video model routing.
- One controlled asset is enough to evaluate visual realism, brand fit, prompt adherence, disclosure needs, and derivative usefulness before automating a batch.
- Real App Store screenshots and later inspected screen capture remain the source of product truth.

## Hard Fails

- visible or simulated Throughline UI;
- credential, account, identifier, notification, or real note content;
- fabricated customer or creator endorsement;
- unsupported “fastest,” native-integration, write-back, or autonomous-action implication;
- asset without a provider/output passport and direct visual review;
- automatic batch continuation after a failed first result.

## Stop Rule

Generate exactly one candidate. Stop after the first job regardless of quality. Record cost, output rights status, visual QA, claim QA, and one reuse decision. A second generation requires a new reviewed prompt or a clear defect correction; it is not an automatic retry loop.
