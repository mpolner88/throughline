# Throughline Creator Brief

**Prepared:** 2026-08-27
**Status:** `planned`; not a publication brief, offer of compensation, request for endorsement, or permission to send.

## Literal Product Loop

Throughline is an iOS voice-note app for an individual who wants a spoken thought to become a structured note with a summary and to-dos, then be readable by their chosen AI agent through an owner-scoped, read-only MCP connection.

`verified_current` evidence checked 2026-08-27: [product charter](../../../docs/PRODUCT.md), [current state](../../../docs/CURRENT_STATE.md), and [agent connection guide](../../../docs/agent-connect.md).

## For And Not For

- **For:** individual builders, operators, writers, and personal-knowledge practitioners who capture thoughts away from a keyboard and already use an AI agent.
- **Not for:** meeting-recording buyers, team-transcription workflows, agent write-back, automatic actions, universal native integrations, or comparative-performance claims.

## Controlled Trial Workflow

1. Use only the synthetic `Return-to-desk plan` from the accepted [creator kit](../proof-factory/creator-kit.md).

3. Inspect a controlled agent retrieval returning only those synthetic tasks.
4. Confirm the visible access boundary: owner-scoped and read-only.
5. Do not display or retain any real notes, credentials, account details, identifiers, or notifications.
