**Prepared:** 2026-08-27
**Slice:** `TL-DISC-001`

## Objective

Learn whether a small, public-evidence-backed group of agent, voice-workflow, and personal-knowledge creators finds the verified Throughline workflow concrete enough to evaluate. This pilot is for qualified discovery and learning, not promised coverage, endorsement, installs, or product outcomes.

## Current Boundary

- `verified_current`: Throughline can turn a voice note into a structured note with a summary and to-dos, and its owner-scoped MCP connection is read-only. Evidence checked 2026-08-27: [current state](../../../docs/CURRENT_STATE.md), [product charter](../../../docs/PRODUCT.md), and [agent connection guide](../../../docs/agent-connect.md).
- `repository_assertion`: the public App Store listing and existing campaign-link package are recorded in the discovery baseline; their account-side campaign state is not reverified here. Evidence checked 2026-08-27: [baseline](../_run/baseline.md) and [campaign links](../../experiments/app-store-campaign-links.csv).
- `verified_current`: root postflight verified the owned discovery [home](https://mpolner88.github.io/throughline/) and [voice-to-task-list page](https://mpolner88.github.io/throughline/voice-to-task-list/) on 2026-08-27. No creator or community draft has been sent.

## Cohort Contract

The first review cohort contains 8 people, below the maximum of 12. It uses these search lenses without forced quotas: five agent builders or MCP educators, one voice-workflow practitioner, and two PKM creators. Every row in [prospect-review.csv](prospect-review.csv) has a dated public source and a public page URL; no direct-contact information is tracked.

Selection is a Mike review only. A selected row does not authorize contact, follow-up, access provisioning, payment, gifting, rights negotiation, account activity, publication, or use of any campaign link.

## Offer And Proof

The prospective offer is a single, controlled review of the `Return-to-desk plan` synthetic workflow from the accepted [creator kit](../proof-factory/creator-kit.md): record a safe spoken plan, inspect one structured note, and verify a read-only retrieval of its three declared tasks. It requests independent judgment and does not ask for favorable coverage.

The selected future destination is the verified public GitHub Pages root plus `/voice-to-task-list/`. Destination readiness does not authorize a send; the proof, campaign label, message, route, disclosure, compensation, rights, and follow-up contract remain item-specific gates.

## Measurement And Learning

- **Primary metric:** Apple first-time downloads attributed to an approved creator source or campaign, only when Apple exposes an aggregate above its reporting threshold.
- **Directional downstream proxy:** same-window aggregate new `auth_succeeded`, then non-demo first value when cohort coverage permits. This is not an install-to-account conversion rate.
- **Learning goal:** aggregate counts by stage plus coded friction or fit themes; retain no correspondence body or product-user material.
- **Coverage gap:** first MCP tool use remains unavailable for reporting, not zero. Evidence checked 2026-08-27: [metrics](../../../product/metrics.md).

## Pilot Flow

1. Mike reviews the scored public cohort and selects individual items.
2. Root verification supplies a real proof asset; the public destination is already verified and must be rechecked before a future send.
3. Mike approves the exact message, route, offer, disclosure, compensation, rights, campaign destination, and one follow-up limit for one named review ID.
4. Mike sends the approved item manually. The tracker records only safe coded states.
5. Stop or defer at the conditions below; interpret only threshold-qualified aggregates.

## Stop Conditions

- Any missing proof, destination, claim, disclosure, compensation, or rights decision.
- Any platform or community rule that is unavailable, prohibits the planned participation, or requires an unapproved prerequisite.
- A requested incentive, gift, paid placement, endorsement, or content reuse right without Mike's exact approval.
- A credible privacy, safety, false-claim, access-control, or product-reliability concern.
- Repeated coded `wrong_audience`, `unclear_value`, or `proof_disbelief` themes across independent approved interactions; this is a messaging/product question, not a conclusion from one reply.
- Apple attribution remains unavailable, an approved campaign is not verified, or public and internal cohorts cannot remain separate.
