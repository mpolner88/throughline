## Rejected language

- `fastest`, `best`, `only`, `seamless`, `native integration`, `write to`, and `automate your life` are excluded from public copy. Comparative speed remains a `hypothesis` without the required evidence and approval.
- No copy claims that an agent understands, remembers, acts correctly, completes tasks, or has a native integration.
