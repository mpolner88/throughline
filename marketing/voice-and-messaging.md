# Throughline voice and messaging

This is the source of truth for marketing agents. When in doubt, follow this before inventing new language.

## Core message

Speak it. Get a plan.

Throughline turns natural voice notes into structured to-dos, summaries, and
ideas—then makes those saved notes available to an AI agent through a private,
read-only MCP endpoint.

## Core loop

1. Record voice notes.
2. Throughline captures them as to-dos, summaries, and memories.
3. Connect MCP directly and get your agent to read them.

## Tone rules

Sound like:

- A sharp builder explaining a real workflow.
- Calm, direct, and specific.
- Curious about how people work.
- Useful before promotional.

Do not sound like:

- A launch hype account.
- A generic SaaS landing page.
- A productivity guru.
- A bot replying to keywords.

Allowed:

- "Lets your agent read notes through MCP."
- "Private, read-only agent access."
- "Not a meeting recorder."

Avoid unless measured:

- "Best."
- "Fastest" in a measured benchmark context. The core message may use "fastest way" as positioning, but do not claim benchmark superiority.
- "Works with every agent."
- "Fully automated."
- "Secure" without explaining the control.

## Objection handling

### "Why not Apple Voice Memos?"

Apple Voice Memos saves audio. Throughline captures the useful parts and exposes the note to your agent.

### "Why not transcription apps?"

Transcription is only the middle step. The point is getting spoken context into the agent workflow.

### "Why MCP?"

Because Throughline should not build every integration. Your agent already connects to tools. Throughline gives the agent your voice notes as context.

### "Is this for meetings?"

No. Throughline is for personal notes: walks, drives, morning planning, reflections, ideas, and messy thoughts.

### "Do agents write back?"

v1 is read-only. Agents can read your notes, summaries, to-dos, and memory. Write-back can come later if the pattern earns it.
