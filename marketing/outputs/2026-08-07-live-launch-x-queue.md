# Throughline live-launch X queue

Status: draft. Human approval is required before any post or reply is published.

Public App Store page: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/throughline-ai-voice-notes/id6774304241

Before publishing, create a separate App Store Connect campaign link for each experiment. Apple campaign attribution requires the campaign and provider tokens generated in App Store Connect; do not invent those parameters by hand.

## Recommended sequence

Use the first four posts across the first week, then let replies and product data determine which of posts 5–7 should follow. Do not publish the whole queue unchanged if the early comments reveal a clearer problem or audience.

## X-LIVE-001 — founder launch

Hypothesis: a specific founder problem will attract higher-intent agent users than a generic product announcement.

Audience: people already using Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, or Cursor for real work.

Asset: 30–45 second proof loop: record → structured note → agent answer.

Post:

> I shipped Throughline.
>
> I built it because a lot of my best work context happens while walking—and when I sit back down, my AI agent knows none of it.
>
> Record a voice note. Turn it into to-dos, summaries, and memory. Connect MCP. Ask your agent later.
>
> https://apps.apple.com/us/app/throughline-ai-voice-notes/id6774304241

Primary signal: qualified replies and App Store campaign product-page views.

Risk: the post needs the proof video; text alone makes the MCP handoff feel abstract.

Review: recommended first post after the proof asset is approved.

## X-LIVE-007 — learning question

Hypothesis: a transparent workflow question will collect better product language than another promotional post.

Audience: existing voice-note and agent users.

Asset: optional screenshot; this should work as text.

Post:

> If you think through work out loud: what happens to those voice notes afterward?
>
> I built Throughline around one answer—turn the note into useful structure and let your agent read it later—but I am curious where this workflow breaks for other people.
>
> What would make you trust or reject a voice → agent loop?

Primary signal: specific objections, trust concerns, and descriptions of existing workflows.

Risk: do not add the App Store link unless someone asks; this post should remain useful as research.

Review: recommended after one proof-oriented launch post.

## Reply workflow

- Read the full post and account context before replying.
- Reply only where Throughline has a genuinely relevant workflow or question.
- Disclose the founder relationship whenever the product is mentioned.
- Prefer useful details, examples, and questions over a link.
- Never automate unsolicited replies, DMs, likes, follows, or mentions.
- Log exact objections and phrases in the experiment ledger; do not paste personal contact details into the repository.
