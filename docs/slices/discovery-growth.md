## Problem

Throughline's public App Store listing communicates the voice-to-structured-task loop, but qualified agent users do not yet have a strong owned proof surface or a repeatable system for finding, producing, testing, and learning from distribution opportunities. The current public website is a support/privacy placeholder rather than a product discovery experience.

## Verified Baseline

- On 2026-08-27 the live US App Store listing showed Throughline version `1.0.4`, the subtitle `Voice to AI Agents & To-Do's`, and the public description of owner-controlled MCP access. Evidence: <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/throughline-ai-voice-notes/id6774304241>.
- On 2026-08-27 the live GitHub Pages root rendered only the Throughline name, one positioning sentence, and privacy/support links. Evidence: <https://mpolner88.github.io/throughline/>.
- On 2026-08-27 the repository current-state source recorded internal TestFlight build `1.0.5 (2026082601)` in the one-tester `Internal QA` group; installation and updated Home-journey use remain unproved. Evidence: [Current State](../CURRENT_STATE.md) and [Home feedback evidence](../evidence/2026-08-27-home-feedback-first-slice.md).
- First MCP tool use remains a measurement coverage gap. Absence of that event must not be reported as zero agent value. Evidence: [Metrics](../../product/metrics.md).

## Outcome

Create a queueable discovery operating system that can produce verified agent-ecosystem packages, an owned product proof surface, reusable proof assets, high-fit creator/community proposals, and demand-capture experiments from a handful of prompts. Every cycle keeps claims, source attribution, experiment IDs, and downstream product outcomes inspectable.

## Positioning

The durable public promise is `voice -> structured notes and to-dos -> readable AI agent`. “The fastest way to get your voice to an AI agent” remains a strategic message hypothesis and is not a comparative public claim until current evidence supports it.

## Scope

- Activate and verify the Sol-directed, Terra-led, Luna-leaf queue.
- Build workstreams 1 through 5 and their evidence manifests.
- Replace the placeholder owned surface with current-build product proof and a named App Store campaign link.
- Produce synthetic, privacy-safe proof assets and channel-native drafts.
- Establish an experiment and learning loop using aggregate, source-labeled evidence.
- Deploy the verified owned surface under the existing reversible GitHub Pages path.

## External Gates

This slice does not independently authorize paid spend, creator compensation, direct outreach, posting from Mike's accounts, community submissions, registry or directory submissions, App Store metadata or submission changes, provider/base-model changes, authentication changes, data-use changes, pricing, recording limits, or onboarding changes. Each such action needs a specific packet containing the exact target, content, cost or commitment, evidence, rollback, and success criterion.

## Metrics

**Primary discovery metric:** first-time App Store downloads attributed to a named source or campaign when Apple exposes the aggregate.

**Qualified-acquisition proxy:** same-window first-time downloads compared with new `auth_succeeded` activity. This is not a matched-user conversion rate.

**Downstream outcomes:** non-demo first value, days 2-7 repeated value, and first MCP tool use when coverage exists.

**Leading indicators:** owned-page App Store click-through, verified listing readiness, proof-asset completion, qualified replies or saves, creator pilot acceptance, and query-level App Store or search visibility when available.

## Guardrails

- Keep public App Store, external TestFlight, internal dogfood, debug, and unknown cohorts separate.
- Do not commit raw audio, transcripts, note text, feedback text, emails, credentials, tokens, contact details, or raw user/session identifiers.
- Generated media may support the story but may not impersonate current product UI.
- Every current fact needs a verification date and evidence link.
- Below-threshold or inaccessible platform data is unavailable, not zero.
- Change one material audience, message, format, or placement variable per experiment.

## Acceptance

- The queue validates and every job has an owner, write scope, dependency, model route, acceptance gate, and external-action boundary.
- Workstreams 1 through 5 produce their declared outputs and evidence manifests.
- The owned page names Throughline, the category, the outcome, and one action in the first viewport.
- Current-build proof uses synthetic data and passes responsive, accessibility, indexing, structured-data, link, and campaign-attribution checks.
- No private content or identifier appears in tracked artifacts or visual frames.
- The integration report separates shipped work, ready action packets, unresolved coverage gaps, and measured results.
