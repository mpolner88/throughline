Three tabs, same hands

Home becomes today, this week and later. Try it: tap a circle, swipe a row to the right, press and hold a row, tap a source label, open notes, record a note, then roll to tomorrow morning.

Scenario

Works exactly as today

  • Tap the circle to complete a task, or tap it again to reopen.
  • Swipe a task right about 76 points: blue ✓ Done, or grey ↺ Reopen on a done task. Same threshold and spring as now.
  • Pull down to refresh; “syncing” shows under the recorder.
  • Note cards keep Read full note, the trash button with “Discard this memory?”, their own to-do circles and the 1–5 grade.
  • The capture tray and recorder behave exactly as in the shipped capture build.

What changes

  • Home is three tabs instead of “most important” over note cards. Tabs switch on tap only, so a sideways swipe always means complete.
  • Note cards move to a notes screen, one tap from the top bar.
  • Completed tasks move into “done today” at the foot of the tab with Undo, instead of striking through in place. They leave at midnight and stay in their note.
  • New, additive: ••• on every row (and the same menu on press and hold) to move a task between tabs or open its note; tapping the source label opens the note; “+n” on a tab when a note lands there.
  • Removed: “unfinished from last night” and the separate “most important” list.
