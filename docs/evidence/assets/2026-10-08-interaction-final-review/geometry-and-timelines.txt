How the data was collected
---------------------------
Geometry comes from a short LLDB attach (sv.lldb). It walks the key
window's views and prints each UIScrollView's window-space top and bottom,
height, contentSize.height and contentOffset.y. Then it detaches. It was
used only on a static screen, between gestures, and never during a
gesture. Tab-band bounds are read from frames against the list's top edge.

"outer" is Home's outer HostingScrollView, "list" is the running-list
scroll view and "tray" is the capture tray's scroll view. Numbers are in
points. "offset" is contentOffset.y; the maximum outer offset is
content - height + bottom inset.

Motion timelines come from two sources:
- `simctl recordVideo` (h264), with frames extracted at 402 px = 402 pt.
  It writes frames only when the screen changes, so frames are sparse.
  Labels are "ms since recording start".
- `cap.sh`: back-to-back JPEG screenshots at about 0.2-0.25 s, each
  labelled with a Unix epoch time.

`grayx` is the leftmost neutral-dark pixel in the first row's band (y 385
pt), which ignores the blue Done reveal. The row is at rest at 24 or 27,
so drag offset = grayx - rest.

Reduce Motion
-------------
Method: the simulator's own Settings app (Accessibility > Motion > Reduce
Motion), driven by simulator-tool taps. Each change was read back with
  xcrun simctl spawn <sim> defaults read com.apple.Accessibility ReduceMotionEnabled
and with a count of the toggle's green pixels. Inside the app it was read
with a brief LLDB attach evaluating UIAccessibility.isReduceMotionEnabled,
then detached before any gesture. No Mac setting was touched.
