Support

Help for Throughline.

Account and data

You can sign out, revoke agent tokens, and delete your account from settings in the app. Deleting your account removes your Throughline account, memories, recordings, feedback, and agent tokens.

Privacy requests

For privacy questions, account deletion issues, or data requests, open a support request through the project issue tracker: github.com/mpolner88/throughline/issues.

App review

Reviewers can use the test account provided in App Store Connect review notes and follow the suggested path there.
