# Throughline launch marketing

This is the launch plan for moving Throughline from App Store submission into a fast marketing iteration loop.

The goal is not one perfect launch post. The goal is a system that ships, measures, learns, and keeps getting sharper.

## Core message

Speak it. Get a plan.

The product story is simple:

1. Record voice notes.
2. Throughline turns them into structured to-dos, summaries, and ideas.
3. Connect the private, read-only MCP endpoint so your agent can read them.

Do not bury the immediate value under infrastructure language. Structured
to-dos are the acquisition hook; MCP is the differentiated second act. The
human promise comes first: speak naturally, get a usable plan, then let your
agent use what you captured.

## Positioning

Primary line:

> Speak it. Get a plan.

Short product description:

> Record a voice note. Throughline turns it into structured to-dos, summaries, and ideas your agent can read through MCP.

Plain-English version:

> Throughline is a voice note app for people who want Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, or another AI agent to know what they said out loud.

Technical version:

> Throughline turns personal voice notes into a private, read-only MCP memory source for your AI agent.

Contrast:

> Voice memo apps save audio. Transcription apps make text. Throughline gets your spoken context into the agent you actually work with.

## Message hierarchy

Use this order everywhere:

1. Speak naturally and get a usable plan.
2. Voice notes become structured to-dos, summaries, and ideas.
3. A private, read-only MCP endpoint lets your agent read saved notes directly.
4. Private, read-only, user-controlled access.
5. Not a meeting recorder.

## Audience wedge

Start narrow.

Primary:

- People already using Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, or other agents in daily work.
- Builders who think out loud on walks, drives, or while pacing around a room.
- People who already have loose voice notes, dictation habits, or second-brain workflows.
- Indie builders, engineers, operators, marketers, writers, and AI power users.

Secondary:

- PKM users who want voice notes to become useful memory.
- MCP experimenters looking for real personal data sources.
- People who want a personal agent workflow without building their own memory stack.

Avoid at launch:

- Meeting recorder buyers.
- Team workspace buyers.
- Generic transcription shoppers.
- Broad productivity app traffic.

## Tone

Throughline should sound like a sharp builder with restraint.

Use:

- Concrete situations: walking, driving, morning planning, late-night thoughts.
- Verbs: record, speak, capture, connect, read, remember, ask.
- Clear product mechanics: voice notes, to-dos, summaries, memories, MCP.
- Plain statements that can survive skeptical technical readers.

Avoid:

- "AI-powered productivity."
- "Revolutionary."
- "Supercharge."
- "Second brain for everything."
- "Automate your life."
- "Seamless integrations."
- "Meeting transcription."

## Launch demo

The strongest asset is a short proof loop:

1. Open Throughline on iPhone.
2. Record a natural voice note: "I am walking back from coffee. Today I need to finish the launch page, email three beta users, and decide whether pricing starts monthly-only."
3. Show Throughline capturing the transcript, to-dos, summary, and memory.
4. Open Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or Codex.
5. Ask: "What did I say I needed to do today?"
6. Show the agent reading the Throughline note through MCP.

Caption:

> The fastest way to get your voice to an AI agent.

The demo should feel real, not overproduced. The magic is the handoff from spoken thought to agent context.

## Channel strategy

### Reddit

Reddit is for community learning, not broadcast.

The agent system should:

- Map each community's rules, norms, allowed self-promotion patterns, and recurring pain points before proposing posts.
- Produce posts as useful artifacts first: workflows, questions, build notes, demos, lessons learned.
- Flag any post that is primarily promotional.
- Track comments as qualitative research, not just engagement.

Best angles:

- "I wanted my agent to remember what I say on walks."
- "I built voice notes as an MCP source."
- "How are people getting voice context into their agents?"
- "This is my current voice -> agent workflow. What would break for you?"

### X / Twitter

X/Twitter is for fast iteration on hooks, demos, and founder-led narrative.

The agent system should:

- Generate many hook variants from the same product truth.
- Keep posts short, specific, and testable.
- Pair most launch posts with a video, screenshot, or concrete workflow.
- Avoid auto-replies, unsolicited mentions, bulk DMs, and engagement automation.

Best angles:

- Founder build story.
- Fast demo.
- Specific agent workflow.
- Before/after: voice memo vs agent-readable memory.
- "I built this because..." narrative.

### Other social channels

Use the same content kernel, but adapt the format:

- LinkedIn: thoughtful founder build note, useful for operators and knowledge workers.
- YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels: screen recording plus voiceover of the loop.
- Product Hunt: only after the landing page and demo are strong.
- Hacker News: technical post about voice notes exposed through MCP.

## Marketing agent system

The full operating system lives in [marketing/README.md](../marketing/README.md).

The launch agents should work as a loop:

1. Signal researcher finds community language, objections, questions, and winning post shapes.
2. Angle strategist turns signals into testable messaging hypotheses.
3. Channel copywriter writes platform-native drafts.
4. Asset director proposes screenshots, short videos, carousels, and diagrams.
5. Community reviewer checks tone, platform rules, claims, and self-promotion risk.
6. Publisher queues approved posts.
7. Performance analyst reads metrics and comments.
8. Learning editor updates the message library and next experiments.

Human approval is required before publishing until a channel has enough evidence and the automation path is policy-safe.

## First experiments

Run small tests fast. Each experiment should test one variable.

### Message tests

- "Fastest way to get your voice to an AI agent."
- "Voice notes your agent can read."
- "Record once. Ask your agent later."
- "Turn messy voice notes into agent memory."
- "Voice -> to-dos, summaries, memories -> MCP."

### Audience tests

- Claude/Codex/Cursor builders.
- MCP tool builders.
- Indie founders.
- PKM users.
- Marketers and operators who dictate ideas.

### Format tests

- 45 second demo.
- 15 second demo.
- Screenshot triptych: record, note, agent answer.
- Plain founder text post.
- Technical diagram.
- Community question.

### Proof tests

- Agent answers from today's voice note.
- Agent pulls to-dos from a morning recording.
- Agent summarizes a week of spoken reflections.
- Agent finds a project mentioned only in a voice note.

## Feedback loop

Every post should produce structured learning.

Capture:

- Channel and community.
- Hook.
- Angle.
- Audience hypothesis.
- Asset type.
- CTA.
- Views, clicks, saves, comments, replies, installs when available.
- Qualitative objections.
- Exact phrases people use.
- Moderator or community friction.
- Next action.

The agent should not simply chase engagement. It should learn which messages create the right behavior: install, record a real note, connect MCP, ask the agent about the note, and come back.

## Success metrics

Primary:

- First recording completion.
- MCP connection started.
- MCP connection completed.
- Second voice note within 48 hours.
- User asks an agent about a Throughline note.

Marketing:

- Hook hold rate on video.
- Replies from target users.
- Useful objections collected.
- App Store product page views.
- Installs by channel.
- Landing page click-through to App Store.

Learning:

- Reusable language discovered.
- Communities that tolerate and value the workflow.
- Content formats that produce installs, not just likes.
- Repeated objections turned into product or copy changes.

## First 10 days

### Before approval

- Build the marketing-agent workspace.
- Produce 30 X/Twitter post variants.
- Produce 15 Reddit-native post drafts, each mapped to a specific community rule set.
- Produce 5 demo scripts.
- Produce 3 static asset concepts.
- Draft the landing page update around the fastest voice-to-agent message.

### Approval day

- Update the landing page with the App Store link.
- Publish the strongest demo on X/Twitter.
- Manually post to one or two high-fit Reddit communities only if the post is useful without the link.
- Send direct outreach to 15 agent-heavy builders.
- Log every reply and objection.

### Days 2-4

- Ship one demo or workflow post per day.
- Test three hooks against the same proof asset.
- Turn comments into new copy.
- Send 10-15 high-fit direct outreach messages per day.
- Add the best phrases to the message library.

### Days 5-10

- Post the technical MCP angle on Hacker News if the demo and setup page are ready.
- Launch Product Hunt only if there is enough proof and a strong landing page.
- Publish a "what I learned from first users" post.
- Decide the next focus: agent builders, PKM users, or operators.
