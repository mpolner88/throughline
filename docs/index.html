Personal by design

For the plans you say out loud.

Use Throughline for personal voice notes while walking, driving, or thinking. It is not a meeting recorder or a replacement for your existing tools.

  • Your note, your connectionYou create the connection that lets an MCP client read your saved notes.
  • Read-only accessThe current agent connection reads saved context. It does not write back or complete tasks.
